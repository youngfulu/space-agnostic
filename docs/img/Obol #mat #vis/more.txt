As part of Obol by Andrius Arutiunian — presented by the Hermès Foundation at Le Forum, Maison Hermès Ginza, Tokyo (Feb. – May 2025) — I collaborated with Parisian artist Alinna Tikhonova on the visual design of shapes and characters inhabiting a world built around bitumen: a petroleum-derived material once sacred, now repurposed as both medium and metaphor.
The exhibition, curated by Tomoya Iwata and conceived as a "club for the dead," unfolds as a speculative descent into the underworld — where ancient cosmologies, esoteric texts and fragments of ritual dissolve the boundaries between past, present and future. Sound installations, a synthetic choir, a laser altar and generative mythological imagery weave through the space, binding playfulness and solemnity into a cold, ritualistic atmosphere. The titular obols — coins placed in the mouths of the dead to pay Charon, ferryman of the underworld — anchor the visual iconography of the entire project.
A work at the intersection of ritual, sound and speculative cosmology — and Arutiunian's first solo exhibition in Japan.
